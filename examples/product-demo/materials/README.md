# Aurel Stoneware

Handmade stoneware mugs for slow coffee and tea at home. Each mug is thrown on the wheel, glazed by hand and fired twice, so no two glazes are the same.

- Three glazes: sage, cream and clay
- 350 ml, keeps coffee warm longer with a double-thick wall
- Dishwasher safe, lead-free food-safe glaze
- Made in a small studio, 40 mugs a week
