# Quartile

Desktop statistics for clinical researchers. Load a spreadsheet, pick an analysis, get a table and a figure you can put in a paper.

- Survival analysis: Kaplan–Meier curves with log-rank test and numbers at risk
- Diagnostic accuracy: ROC curves with AUC and 95% confidence interval
- Meta-analysis: forest plots with fixed and random effects and I² heterogeneity
- Every result exports as a reproducible R script, so a reviewer can rerun it
- Figures export at 300 or 600 DPI
- Runs on your own Mac or Windows PC; your data never leaves the computer
- A phone companion lists your saved analyses
