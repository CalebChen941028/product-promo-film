# Larchwood

Three small cabins on a lake, for people who want a few days without a screen.

- Three cabins on the shore of Lake Øyvatn, Telemark, Norway; each sleeps two
- A 40-minute walk from the nearest road; your bags go over on a hand cart
- No Wi-Fi; there is phone signal only on the jetty
- A wood-fired sauna on the shore, and the lake to cool off in
- Solar power, a wood stove and a kettle; drinking water from the spring
- Open 15 May to 30 September; stays of three nights or more
- Book at larchwood.example
