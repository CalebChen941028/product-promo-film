## 2. Strategist · 策略
- Audience truth · 受众真相: I want the air in my room to feel softer, without thinking about it.
- Insight · 洞察: the room is always there; the moment you notice it is when the air is dry and nothing is wrong.
- Single-minded proposition · 唯一主张: Soft mist for the room you live in.
- Proof · 证据: mist rises from the top of a frosted dome; a soft blue-violet light glows inside; the same unit sits in a study, a living room and outdoors.

## 3. Creative director · 创意总监
- Concept A: "The mist keeps rising" — the film is one slow mist plume seen from a reader who never looks up; the room carries on — opening image: a woman reading at a window, mist rising beside her — why it fits: calm, specific, and true to the photo.
- Concept B: "A quiet room" — the room itself is the subject, every frame a different corner of home — opening image: a sunlit sofa with a blanket — why it fits: atmosphere, but it describes the room, not the product.
- Concept C: "Lit from within" — built on the glow inside the dome — opening image: the product on white, light coming up through the frost — why it fits: strong object shot, but it is only one frame of the film.
- Chosen · 选定: A. It is the most true (the photos show the mist rising) and the most surprising (the product is never the loudest thing on screen; the reader's calm is).
- Hook (0–3 s) · 开场钩子: the reader at a window, the mist plume rising beside her; she keeps reading.
- Hero moment · 高潮时刻: the product alone on a white stage, its light coming up through the frost while the mist rises; held for about 3 s, at about 65% of the film.
- Hook line · 开场句: The reader never looks up. The mist keeps rising.
- Hero line · 高潮句: Humidifier. Soft mist, quiet room.
- Last line · 收尾句: The mist keeps rising.
- Title · 片名: The mist keeps rising
